Project process and remote collaboration
  1. Discuss the context

    Goals, existing system, timeline and team constraints.

  2. Agree the scope

    Deliverables, risks, acceptance criteria and a proposal.

  3. Deliver in milestones

    Review working increments and record decisions.

  4. Verify and hand over

    Code, documentation, deployment notes and remaining limitations.
