CiftoPay · Aggregated payments for local businesses

A payment and digitalization service for merchants in Sanmenxia, China. Aggregated QR payments are the core; partner software covers restaurants, retail, utility bill payment, schools, parking and hotels, with custom development around payments. Funds are settled directly by licensed payment institutions — the platform never holds them. It serves merchants (QR codes and smart speakers), sales agents (profit-sharing back office) and developers (open API).

My role
Architect, full-stack (2026.06 – present). Overall architecture; payment orchestration, channel adapters, double-entry ledger and daily reconciliation; three web portals and four mobile apps.
Key design
Persist-before-call payment flow; unknown results are only queried, never re-sent; transactional outbox + RocketMQ into an append-only double-entry ledger; one codebase deployable as a monolith or microservices.

ECP · Overseas payment platform for the Indian market

Local pay-in and pay-out in India for merchants: pay-in via UPI and Net Banking, pay-out via UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS. The platform covers merchant onboarding and fees, accounts and double-entry bookkeeping, settlement batches and reconciliation, risk lists and disputes, with an operations console, a merchant portal and a signed merchant API.

My role
Remote part-time contract · architecture and full-stack development. Service boundaries and payment-domain layering, the trade / ledger / settlement core and both Vue 3 consoles.
Key design
HMAC-SHA256 / RSA2 signatures with nonce replay protection and idempotency keys; explicit state machines with optimistic locking; multi-leg double entry in integer minor units (total = available + frozen); settlement batches on Indian bank working days.
